Teenage life is a pivotal phase of rapid physical, mental, and social development. It's a time of figuring out your identity, navigating relationships, and managing peer pressure. Because your brain's prefrontal cortex is still developing, processing emotions and controlling impulses can sometimes be challenging.

My Adulthood

Adulthood is a beautiful, messy, and continuous process of figuring things out as you go. It is largely defined by taking responsibility for your own path, managing finances, making everyday choices, and embracing growth rather than striving for a rigid or "perfect" life timeline.

My Favorite Food

This is a Tortang Talong, My Favorite Food. Tortang talong, also known as eggplant omelette, is an omelette or fritter from Filipino cuisine made by pan-frying grilled whole eggplants dipped in an egg mixture. It is a popular breakfast and lunch meal in the Philippines.
